# Assets

**Art direction:** Premium neon arcade platformer for a younger audience: midnight navy sky, electric cyan and hot pink highlights, luminous emerald platforms, gold collectibles, glassmorphism HUD cards, crisp readable silhouettes, layered parallax scenery, and celebratory particle bursts. The game should feel energetic, playful, and polished without relying on a copied franchise art style.

| File | Role | Generation brief | Runtime use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `art-direction-reference.png` | 16:9 visual target | In-game screenshot reference with neon sky, glowing platforms, gold coins, original caped hero silhouette, mushroom-like enemies, star goal, and HUD blocks; no readable text or logos. | Start overlay visual direction card |
| `princess.png` | Transparent character art | Original joyful princess with violet hair, magenta/gold crown, cyan/pink dress, isolated cutout, no text or logo. | Victory overlay; CSS dance animation |

Gameplay art is deterministic canvas art: the player, enemies, platforms, boxes, coins, goal flag, particles, parallax mountains, clouds, and stars are drawn at runtime for crisp scaling and low load cost.
